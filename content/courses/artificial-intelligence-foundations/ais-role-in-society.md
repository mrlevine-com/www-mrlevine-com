### AI in the Community
With the help of the articles on Code.org level 1 or your own research and the AI chat on level 2, record your answers to the questions below for each of the following AI uses in different community contexts:

- AI in Grocery Stores
- AI in Medical Diagnoses
- AI in Public Transportation
- AI in Schools

Questions:

- Who in the community benefits most from this AI? How do they benefit?
- Who in the community is most harmed from the use of this AI? How are they harmed?
- Are there any negative effects or unintended consequences, such as environmental impact?
