## Today's Objectives
- Explain the roles of hardware, software, and operating systems in executing tasks.
- Justify computer system design decisions based on the resources available and intended use of the system.

## Lesson Overview
### What is a PC build?
{{% define "PC Build" %}}

Today, you'll design a PC build for a fictional user. You and a partner will research real hardware on PCPartPicker.com and use an AI Computer Build Assistant chatbot to help you choose components that fit your user's needs, budget, and preferences.

## Assignment
{{% instructions-code-org-update %}}
{{% instructions-unit-journal-update %}}

### Choose a User
Go to Code.org level 1 and select a user profile to design a computer system for. Record the following about your user:

- Name
- Job or hobby
- Purpose (what they need the computer to do)
- Budget
- Preferences and additional info

### Choose Your Roles
With your partner, decide who will start in each role below. Switch roles halfway through the activity so you both get practice in each one:

- **AI Manager**: Leads the conversation with the chatbot, reads prompts aloud and checks with your partner before submitting, and summarizes chatbot responses for the team.
- **Researcher**: Uses PCPartPicker to check prices, specs, and compatibility; identifies trade-offs and checks the chatbot's advice against real specs and prices; records prompts, refinements, and trade-offs.

### Select Your Hardware
Go to Code.org level 2 to explore PCPartPicker.com. Then go to level 3 and use PCPartPicker together with the Computer Build Assistant chatbot to select hardware that fits your user's needs, budget, and compatibility requirements. For each part below, record what you chose, its price, and a trade-off you considered:

- Case
- Motherboard
- Storage
- Operating system
- Peripherals (e.g. monitor, keyboard, mouse, webcam)

Record the total price of your build.

### Practice Iterative Design
Take a screenshot of your build from PCPartPicker and upload it to the Computer Build Assistant. Ask the chatbot a question about your build, such as "What are the main strengths of this build for my user?", "Can you check it for compatibility?", or "Do any parts need to be upgraded or changed?"

1. Record your initial prompt and the chatbot's response.
2. Rate the response from 1 (not clear/not useful) to 5 (very clear/very useful).
3. Revise your prompt to be clearer or more specific about your user's needs and preferences, then submit it again.
4. Record the new response and rate it again.
5. Note what worked or didn't work when you revised your prompt, and update your build based on the feedback you received.
