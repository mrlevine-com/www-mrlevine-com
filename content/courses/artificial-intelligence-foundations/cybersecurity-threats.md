## Lesson Overview
### Would you pay to get your files back?
Imagine someone installs software on your device that locks your photos, school projects, notes, and saved passwords. You can't open any of them unless you pay a large ransom.

{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

There's no right answer, but this attack is real. It's called **ransomware**, and it can take away:

- **Functionally:** your schoolwork, passwords, and schedules.
- **Emotionally:** your photos and memories.

### How do the four threats attack a system?
![Four threats. DDoS: many devices flood one server with traffic. Misconfiguration: a file with an open lock. Worm: one infected device copies itself to two others. Ransomware: a file with a closed lock.](/images/cyber-threat-effects.svg)

{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

Each one exploits a **vulnerability**, a weak spot, to disrupt access, corrupt data, or gain control.

| Threat               | What it does                                     | Effect           | Example                                                      |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| **DDoS**             | Floods a site with traffic from many devices     | Disrupts access  | A college application site crashes on deadline day          |
| **Misconfiguration** | Leaves settings too open, so strangers get in    | Corrupts data    | An essay set to "Anyone with the link can edit" fills with SpongeBob quotes |
| **Worm**             | Malware that copies itself to other devices      | Gains control    | A "free V-Bucks" link emails itself to all your contacts   |
| **Ransomware**       | Malware that locks (encrypts) files until you pay | Disrupts access | A hospital can't open patient records                        |

DDoS stands for **Distributed Denial of Service**.

### How does AI make these threats worse?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

It helps attackers go faster and bigger.

- **Speed:** AI can scan thousands of systems for weak settings in minutes.
- **Scale:** AI can aim attacks at many targets at once, tailoring each one.
- **Stealth:** AI can keep changing malware so security tools miss it.
- **Defense:** AI also helps defenders spot strange activity and block it early.

{{</ collapse >}}
