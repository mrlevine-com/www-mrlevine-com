## Lesson Overview
### What does cybersecurity protect you from?

- A text never sends.

### Why did Jordan's day go wrong?
Jordan, a 10th grader, has a paper, a quiz, and a meeting today. Then:

1. **Login:** his password stops working, and his account locks.
2. **File:** his assignment link opens another student's work.
3. **Wi-Fi:** it crashes mid-quiz, and his answers are gone.
4. **Group chat:** his message never sends, so he misses the meeting.

![Three causes of failure. Technical breakdown, like Wi-Fi crashing mid-quiz. User error, like typing the wrong password. Poor design, like a quiz that doesn't autosave.](/images/why-systems-fail.svg)

{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the causes." >}}

Most failures have more than one cause:

| Failure    | Cause                                                      |
|------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| Login      | User error (wrong password) or technical breakdown          |
| File       | Poor design (anyone with the link can open it)              |
| Wi-Fi      | Technical breakdown and poor design (no autosave)           |
| Group chat | Technical breakdown and poor design (no "not sent" warning) |

{{</ collapse >}}

### How did the failures affect Jordan?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

In two ways:

- **Functionally:** he couldn't turn in his paper, finish his quiz, or get to his meeting.
- **Emotionally:** he felt frustrated, stressed, and left out.

{{</ collapse >}}
