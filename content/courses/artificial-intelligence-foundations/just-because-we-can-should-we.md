## Lesson Overview
### What rules or values should guide how we design and use emerging technologies?
{{% define "Ethics" %}}

Watch on YouTube: [Ethics & AI: Privacy & the Future of Work](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zNxw5gJtHLc)

AI can raise ethical concerns, especially when it collects personal data or replaces human workers. When you judge whether a technology is ethical, check the following six areas:

| Area | Ask yourself |
|---|---|
| Privacy | Is people's personal data protected? |
| Transparency | Can people see how the system makes decisions? |
| Fairness | Does it treat every group of people equally? |
| Bias | Could it favor some people over others? |
| Control | Can people challenge or override its decisions? |
| Consent | Did people agree to have their data used? |
