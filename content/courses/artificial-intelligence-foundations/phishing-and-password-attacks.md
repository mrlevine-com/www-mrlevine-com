### What could someone do with what you post online?

Guess your passwords, or write messages that feel made just for you.

- **Passwords:** pets, birthdays, and favorite teams are easy to find and easy to guess.
- **Messages:** an attacker who knows your interests can fake a message you'd want to answer.

Even secure systems break down when attackers target the people using them.

### How do you spot phishing?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

Look at what the message wants you to do, not at its grammar. AI can write perfect, personal messages, and thousands of them at once.

- **Urgency:** "Reply by tonight" or "Your account closes in 24 hours."
- **Too good to be true:** free tickets, merch, or early access.
- **Too personal:** details pulled from your own posts.
- **Fake links:** web addresses that look almost right, like `school.edu-login.info`.
- **The ask:** your birthday, address, password, or a click.

{{</ collapse >}}

{{% define "Phishing" %}}

### How do attackers break into accounts?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

Mostly through predictable habits: weak passwords, personal info, and reusing passwords.

| Attack                  | How it works                                                  | Clue                                       |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| **Password guessing**   | Tries different passwords on one account                      | A few failed tries, then a personal password |
| **Brute force**         | Uses automated tools to rapidly try huge numbers of passwords on one account | Hundreds of fast tries             |
| **Password spraying**   | Tries one common password on many accounts to avoid detection | Many accounts hit in a short time          |
| **Credential stuffing** | Uses real usernames and passwords from past breaches on other accounts | Works on the first try             |

{{</ collapse >}}

### How do you get through today's levels?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

1. Level 1: pick a user and read their posts. Use the same user in every level. When a level mentions an activity guide, do "Investigate Your User" in your Unit Journal instead.
2. Level 2: read the three messages sent to your user, and decide which are real and which are phishing.
3. Level 3: ask AI Chat which messages were phishing and what made them believable.
4. Level 4 (Check for Understanding): decide whether "This message is phishing because it has bad grammar" is complete, incomplete, or incorrect, and explain why.
5. Level 5: read your user's breach report, a record of how someone got into their account without permission, and decide which password attack was used.
