### AI Time Capsule
Record a presentation explaining how you collaborated with AI to shape and improve your final prediction.

Refer to the following rubric for guidance:

- **Evaluating AI-Generated Predictions**: You must accurately identify unrealistic, incorrect, or hallucinated elements in AI outputs and refine them using evidence and reasoning.
- **Multimodal Input Exploration**: You must use multiple input types (e.g. text, image, PDF) and explain how each one contributes to the AI output or product. The use of inputs must be clearly documented and connected to decisions made during the process.
- **Prompt Iteration and Refinement**: You must revise prompts multiple times, with changes that result in clearly improved or more targeted AI responses. Revisions must show increasing control over the content or quality of AI output.
- **Bias & Ethical Awareness**: You must identify bias, missing perspectives, or potential harms in AI outputs and makes specific changes to improve fairness, representation, or ethical responsibility.
- **Reflection and Process Awareness**: You must describe multiple decisions made while working with AI and explain how those decisions affected your prediction or presentation. Reflection must include what you learned about AI’s strengths and limitations.
- **Presentation and Synthesis**: Final product must explain the steps you took to create and refine your prediction using AI. The presentation must include specific examples of prompt changes, decisions made, and how you shaped or revised the output. You must clearly revise AI-generated content and present it in your own voice.
