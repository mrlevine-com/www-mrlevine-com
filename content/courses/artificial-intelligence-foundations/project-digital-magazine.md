## Today's Objectives
- Apply a consistent brand identity across all pages using a shared stylesheet.
- Plan a multi-page site by decomposing it into a site map, wireframes, and a defined user and purpose.
- Reflect on how AI supported and influenced their design decisions throughout the project.
- Test and refine their site for accessibility and responsiveness using the POUR framework and peer feedback.
- Use AI prompts to generate and iterate on their site across multiple milestones.

## Lesson Overview
### What is a digital magazine?
**A multi-page website that looks and feels like a magazine**, built around one theme for one specific reader. Over the next three days, you'll build your own with at least two pages.

| Day | Milestone | What you'll do |
|-----|-----------|----------------|
| 1 | **Plan** (Levels 1–6) | Choose a theme, then create a brand kit, site map, and wireframes for at least two pages |
| 2 | **Build** (Levels 7–9) | Build a first draft with AI, then get feedback from a classmate |
| 3 | **Finalize** (Levels 10–12) | Self-assess with the rubric, fix your top issues, then share your magazine |

Your first draft doesn't need to be perfect, and you don't need to fix everything on Day 3. Focus on what matters most for your reader.

### How will your project be graded?
| Criteria | Full credit looks like |
|----------|------------------------|
| Decomposition and Design Planning | A site map of every page and wireframes for at least two pages, all tied to your reader and purpose |
| Designing for Consistency | Colors, fonts, and layout match your theme on every page |
| Accessibility | Every image has alt text, text has strong contrast, and you used POUR to fix at least one issue |
| Responsiveness | Pages work on at least two screen sizes, and you fixed at least one issue you found by testing |
| Reflection and Iteration with AI | Your iteration log shows at least two times you changed AI's output, including one where you overrode it |

### How do you give feedback?
Use TAG:

- **Tell** them something you like.
- **Ask** them a question.
- **Give** a suggestion for improvement.
