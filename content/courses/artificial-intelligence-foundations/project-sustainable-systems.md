## Today's Objectives
- Demonstrate how hardware, software, and communication combine to create a computer system.
- Design a prototype of a device that solves a real-world issue.
- Evaluate the ethical implications of design decisions.

## Lesson Overview
### How can you design a computer system to solve a real-world problem?
Over the next three days, you will design an AI-infused computer system that helps achieve one of the United Nations [Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)](https://sdgs.un.org/goals). Your final product is a **system diagram** of your system, with descriptions, labels, and reflections.

### How will your project be graded?
| Criteria | Full credit looks like |
|---|---|
| System Diagram | A clear diagram showing user interactions, data flow, processing, and a problem statement |
| Hardware Layer | Names the hardware in your system and explains how it works with the other layers |
| Software Layer | Explains how software runs key functions and processes data (e.g. automation, AI, interactive features) |
| Communications Layer | Explains in detail how your system sends and receives data across devices, networks, or the cloud |
| Ethical Considerations | Reflects on ethical issues in your system and proposes ways to fix them in the future |

## Assignment

### Day 1: Explore the Goals (~5mins)
Browse the [17 Sustainable Development Goals](https://sdgs.un.org/goals). Record which goals are personally relevant or interesting to you, and why.

### Day 1: Choose an SDG Target (~10mins)
Complete Level 1 on Code.org. Record three targets you are interested in. Then choose **one** target to focus on and record it.

### Day 1: Research Your Target (~10mins)
Complete Level 2 on Code.org. Record notes under four headings: **Examples**, **Facts**, **Real-World Challenges**, and **Project Ideas**. Refine at least one prompt to get a more specific answer, and record both versions.

### Day 1: Describe Your System (~5mins)
Choose **one** of your project ideas. Record a 2 to 3 sentence description of the computer system you will design.

### Day 1: Create Two User Personas (~5mins)
Complete Level 3 on Code.org to create your first user persona, then ask the AI what is missing from that persona for a focus area you choose (e.g. "What might be missing from this persona if I want to make my system more sustainable?"). Use its answer to create a second persona. For each persona, record:

- **Description**: Who they are and what they need
- **Challenges**: 2 to 3 problems your system could help them solve

### Day 1: Sketch a Rapid Prototype (~5mins)
Record which types of data your system needs: audio, visual, biometric, text, location, and/or environmental. Then sketch your device and label its key parts. Don't worry about details yet.

### Day 1: List Your Components (~5mins)
Record the components your system needs, grouped like this:

- **Hardware**
    - **Input & Sensors** (e.g. camera, chemical sensor, weight sensor)
    - **Processing** (e.g. CPU, GPU)
    - **Storage & Memory** (e.g. SSD, cloud, RAM)
    - **Output** (e.g. screen, motor, speaker)
- **Communication** (e.g. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, 5G)
- **Software** (e.g. app, operating system, AI)

### Day 2: Plan Your Data Flow (~5mins)
Record answers to these five questions:

1. **Goal:** What need will your system address?
2. **Data:** What data will your system use?
3. **Action:** Which verb will your system apply to the data? Choose from: aggregate, compare, filter, group, match, sort, summarize, or validate.
4. **Output:** What will the output look like?
5. **Insight:** What will the output help people learn or decide?

### Day 2: Write a Problem Statement (~5mins)
Record a problem statement using your Data Flow answers:

- The system needs to **[goal]**.
- To do this, it will **[action]** **[data]** by **[specific condition]**.
- The system will then produce **[output]** so that **[insight or decision]**.

### Day 2: Build Your System Diagram (~30mins)
Draw a system diagram. Include:

1. Your SDG target
2. Your problem statement, labeled **System Description**
3. A numbered diagram showing how data moves from the user, through your hardware, software, and communication, to the output
4. A short label on every part explaining what it does

Check your diagram against the rubric in the Lesson Overview. A diagram that meets every criterion beats a "perfect" one that doesn't.

### Day 2: Reflect on Your Users (~5mins)
Record how one of your personas would use your system to solve a problem.

### Day 3: Analyze Ethics and Impact (~5mins)
Complete Level 4 on Code.org. Then add an **Ethical Considerations** section to your diagram that names the concerns you found and how you would fix them.

### Day 3: Get AI Feedback (~5mins)
Complete Level 5 on Code.org, asking for feedback based on the rubric in the Lesson Overview. Record the feedback and make **one** key improvement to your diagram.

### Day 3: Reflect on Working With AI (~5mins)
Add a **Reflection: AI As Co-Creator** section to your diagram describing one AI suggestion you accepted, one you rejected, and why. Then check your diagram against the rubric one last time.
