## Lesson Overview
### What is software?
Hardware and software work as a team. Hardware performs the physical tasks; software performs the mental ones. Every computer system relies on three types of software:

- **System software** manages hardware and other software and enables basic functionality (e.g. Android, Windows, iOS, etc.).
- **Application software** helps users perform specific tasks (e.g. TikTok, Chrome, Instagram, etc.).
- **Utility software** maintains, optimizes, and secures the system (e.g. antivirus tools, disk cleanup, etc.).

### Software Scavenger Hunt
With a partner, search your iPad or computer to find real examples of operating systems, application software, and utility software. For each task, record your answer to the question and include a screenshot of where you found the software (make sure no personal information is visible).

#### Operating Systems
1. **Identify your OS**: Find where your device shows the name of its operating system. What does this operating system allow your device to do?
2. **Manage System Settings**: Open the settings menu and explore the network and privacy options. What are two examples of tasks you can manage in this menu, and how do they help protect the user?

#### Application Software
3. **Productivity Tools**: Find an app that helps you create, edit, and format a document. What tasks can this app help you accomplish, and why is it useful?
4. **Entertainment**: Find a gaming app or other entertainment software. How does this software make use of your device's hardware (e.g. graphics card, speakers) to create an immersive experience?
5. **Communication**: Find an app used for communication. How might this app help you collaborate effectively with others?

#### Utility Software
6. **System Maintenance Tools**: Find a utility program that helps clean up disk space or manage files. Why is this tool important for maintaining a computer's performance?
7. **Battery Performance**: Locate where your device shows battery health or performance statistics. How can this information help you troubleshoot issues or prevent problems?
8. **Software Updates**: Find where your device shows information about software updates. Why is it important to keep software updated, and how does it affect performance and security?

#### Bonus
9. **Software and Hardware**: Find an example of software that directly interacts with hardware. How does this software enable the hardware to function?
10. **Comparing Software**: Find two different apps that perform similar tasks. What are the advantages of using one over the other?
