### How does AI generate responses?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

AI generates responses by (in simple terms):

1. Reading your prompt
2. Predicting likely responses based on patterns in its training data
3. Choosing a response based on probability and randomness

{{% define "Probability" %}}

### Can AI make mistakes?

{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

Yes, AI can make mistakes because it turns your prompt into a simplified representation, which can cause important details to be lost in the process. This abstraction can lead to misunderstandings and incorrect responses.

{{% define "Abstraction" %}}
