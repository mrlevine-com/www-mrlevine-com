## Lesson Overview
### What is human-centered design?
{{% define "Human-Centered Design" %}}

Modern Amazon is a good example:

- **Simple categories** help you find what you need quickly.
- **Large images** help you see what you're getting before you buy.
- **Recommendations** help you discover things that match your interests.

### What is user experience?
{{% define "User Experience" %}}

Good user experience (UX) means a product is easy to use, efficient, and enjoyable.

### What does a UX designer do?
UX designers create products that give users meaningful, smooth, and relevant experiences. They:

- Research how people behave, so important buttons are right where you expect them.
- Test apps from a specific user's point of view.
- Identify what's working for that user and what isn't.
- Make specific recommendations backed by what they noticed.
- Make sure technology works for everyone, including people with different physical or cognitive abilities.

### How do UX designers know what users need?
{{% define "User Profile" %}}

A user profile helps you understand a user's goals, needs, and behaviors. Judge a design by "this doesn't work for my user because...", not "I personally find this confusing." An app can be easy for one user and hard for another.
