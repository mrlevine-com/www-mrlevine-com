## Lesson Overview
### Why can AI be biased?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

AI can be biased because it learns from patterns in data, but that data isn't always balanced or fair. If certain perspectives or groups are overrepresented, AI will reflect those patterns in its responses. This can lead to AI making assumptions that don’t accurately represent the full picture.

### How can you reduce AI bias?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

You can:

- Improve training data to be more representative.
- Use prompting strategies to mitigate bias.
