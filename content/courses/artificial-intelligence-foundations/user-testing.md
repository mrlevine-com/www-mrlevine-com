## Lesson Overview
### How can user testing help make a product better? What happens when user testing is skipped?
{{% define "User Testing" %}}

Watch on YouTube: [Guerilla Testing with Usability Cafe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0YL0xoSmyZI)

**User testing helps because it:**

- Identifies hidden problems
- Improves user experience
- Saves time and money in the long run
- Builds empathy with users
- Leads to new innovation

**Skipping it risks:**

- Products that don't work
- Frustrated users
- Damage to the company's reputation
- Missed ideas

**Working with the data:** You'll see observations, survey responses, and feedback. AI can spot patterns fast. Just check it for accuracy.

**Deciding what's next:** A team can launch with no action, launch and fix, delay to test more, or cancel the project. There's no single right answer. Justify your choice and think about who it affects.
