## Lesson Overview
### What is machine learning?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

Machine learning is when a computer recognizes patterns and makes decisions without being explicitly programmed.

Today you'll train one to tell fish apart from trash, so it can help clean the ocean.

{{</ collapse >}}

### How does a machine "learn"?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

A machine learns from examples:

1. You label examples, like "fish" or "not fish." These examples are the training data.
2. The computer finds patterns in your training data.
3. The computer uses those patterns to label new images on its own.

Your labels are programming the computer, even though you aren't writing code.

{{</ collapse >}}

### How does bias get into machine learning?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

A model learns the opinions of whoever made its training data, so it learns their biases too.

- **Collecting more data won't fix it.** Bias comes from the type or source of the data.
- **Diverse training data helps.**
- **Empathy helps.** Think about who your work affects, and make sure no group is left out.

{{</ collapse >}}
