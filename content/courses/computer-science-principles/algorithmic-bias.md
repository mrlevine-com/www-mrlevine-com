## Lesson Overview
### Which tasks shouldn't an algorithm do?
Read [Twitter says its image-cropping algorithm was biased, so it's ditching it](https://www.cnn.com/2021/05/19/tech/twitter-image-cropping-algorithm-bias/index.html).

{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

Ones best done by people, like choosing how to show yourself.

- **What went wrong:** Twitter tested its algorithm and found its crops had gender and race biases.
- **Twitter's fix:** it stopped using the algorithm and let people crop their own photos.

Twitter's Rumman Chowdhury, who led its machine learning ethics team, explained:

> "One of our conclusions is that not everything on Twitter is a good candidate for an algorithm, and in this case, how to crop an image is a decision best made by people."
