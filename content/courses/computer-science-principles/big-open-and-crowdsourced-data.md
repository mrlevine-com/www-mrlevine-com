### Why does big data need more than one computer?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

When data gets too big, one computer can't process it all.

- **The more data, the more you learn:** businesses and scientists keep working with bigger and bigger datasets.
- **Share the work:** cloud computing and parallel systems split the data across many computers.
- **Plan to grow:** a scalable system keeps working as you add more and more data.

{{</ collapse >}}

### How do many people help collect data?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

Through crowdsourcing and citizen science, computers let people collaborate to solve problems.

- **Crowdsourcing:** like a map app that learns about traffic jams from drivers' phones.
- **Citizen science:** like volunteers who use their phones to report on local streams for scientists.
- **Crowdfunding:** a kind of crowdsourcing that connects businesses or social causes with money from many people.

{{</ collapse >}}

### Why share data openly?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

So more people can use it to learn and solve problems.

- **Open data:** data that governments, organizations, and others make available to the public.
- **Impact on science:** shared data spreads useful knowledge and lets others use it to make new discoveries.
