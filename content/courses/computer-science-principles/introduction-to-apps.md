## Lesson Overview
### What are apps, and how do you interact with them?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

Apps are designed to solve a problem by providing you with a service. For example:

| Category             | Examples                     |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------|
| Entertainment          | Movies, TV shows, games      |
| Social                 | Social media, chatting       |
| Commerce               | Marketplaces                 |
| Finding Information    | Search engines, maps         |

Apps have a screen, or user interface, that displays and collects information to and from you.

{{% define "User Interface" %}}

Things like buttons, sliders, and text boxes collect information from you. Sounds, images, video, and text display information to you.

{{</ collapse >}}

### What are the inputs and outputs of an app?
{{% define "Input" %}}
{{% define "Output" %}}
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal examples." >}}

An input could be you clicking a button or tapping the screen. An output could be an image displayed or a sound played.

The output of an app is usually based on its inputs, or on the way the app was set up to begin with. For example, an app could be programmed to play a song as soon as it opens.

{{</ collapse >}}

### App Exploration
With a partner, explore five sample apps on Code.org:

- **Water Conservation Tips**: teaches you ways to conserve water by clicking through screens and making choices, with images or sounds that play to show whether a choice is correct.
- **Bird Quiz**: quizzes you on trivia about birds; answer three questions correctly to reach the winning screen, or answer incorrectly and start over.
- **Hamilton Township Improvement Project**: educates you about steps your township is taking to beautify the town.
- **4-Square = 4 Times the Fun**: teaches you the rules of the game four square, including how many people are needed to play.
- **Monarch Butterflies**: lets you click through screens and pictures to explore the life stages of the monarch butterfly.

Choose one app and record the following in your journal:

- How do you interact with the app?
- What is the overall purpose of the app?
- Who is the target audience?

### App Investigation
Return to the sample apps and consider their inputs and outputs. Record the following in your journal:

- What input does the app collect from you?
- What output does the app display or play?
