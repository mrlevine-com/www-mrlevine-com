## Today's Objectives
- Examine the effects of lossy compression on text and images.
- Given a piece of media, decide whether to use lossy or lossless compression based on the needs of a situation.

## Lesson Overview
### Vocabulary
- **Lossy Compression**: A process for reducing the number of bits needed to represent something in which some information is lost or thrown away (this process is not reversible)

### How do you decide whether to use lossless or lossy compression?
{{< collapse summary="Click here to reveal the answer." >}}

- Lossless compression is useful when the accuracy of the original item is most important (e.g. bank records, text files, some images).
- Lossy compression is useful when file size is a concern or when it needs to be sent in a reasonable amount of time (e.g. multimedia - especially streaming media (images, video, audio)).
