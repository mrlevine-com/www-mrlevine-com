## Today's Objectives
- Identify how an internet dilemma has the potential to benefit and harm different stakeholders.
- Identify the ways the technical structure and design of the Internet contributes to a social dilemma.

## Lesson Overview
### What have I learned so far?

- The physical Internet, and protocols like IP, TCP, and UDP

## Assignment
{{% instructions-unit-journal-update %}}

### Step 1: Choose Your Dilemma (Day 1)
You are the Chief Technology Advisor for a candidate running for elected office. Your candidate wants your help understanding an important technology dilemma and coming up with a policy recommendation. Choose **one** of the following dilemmas to investigate:

| Dilemma | Core Question |
|---------|----------------|
| **Net Neutrality** | When and how should internet service providers be allowed to treat some kinds of internet traffic different from others? |
| **Internet Censorship** | When and how should the government be allowed to censor or block internet traffic, if at all? |
| **The Digital Divide** | When and how should resources be invested to close gaps between those who do and don't use the Internet? |

Record which dilemma you chose and its core question.

{{% define "Digital Divide" %}}

### Step 2: Find and Review Your Sources (Day 1)
Find at least 3 sources online about your dilemma (articles, videos, or Wikipedia pages all count). For each source, record:

- The title and a link to the source
- Instances where your dilemma's impacted groups are mentioned
- Technical details that are explained

### Step 3: Write Your Policy Report (Day 2)
Add the following to your Unit Journal:

- **Impacted Groups**: For each group, describe who they are and explain their interests, benefits, and harms related to the dilemma.
- **Technical Background**: For each technical concept that applies, explain what it means in your own words and why it matters for your dilemma.
- **Recommended Policy Solution**: State a clear policy recommendation, and list who benefits and who is harmed by it.

### Step 4: Learn the Other Dilemmas (Day 2)
Meet with classmates who chose each of the other 2 dilemmas. For each one, record a brief summary in your Unit Journal:

- The core question
- Their policy recommendation

You should leave with a full picture of all 3 dilemmas, not just your own.
