## Today's Objectives
- Create an effective visualization.
- Describe new insights or decisions that can be made based on a visualization.
- Explain information in a visualization.
- Follow the Data Analysis Process to tell a data story.
- Recognize and explain potential bias in a dataset or interpretation.
- Write a short explanation of a data set referencing the metadata.

## Lesson Overview
### What will you do?
**Tell a story with data.** You'll pick a dataset from App Lab's Data Library, chart it in the Data Visualizer, and explain what your chart teaches you.

![A cycle of five steps: 1. Ask Questions, 2. Collect/Choose Data, 3. Clean/Filter Data, 4. Visualize Data, 5. Generate New Information, then back to 1.](/images/data-analysis-process.svg)

| Day | Steps                                               | Code.org Level |
|-----|-----------------------------------------------------|----------------|
| 1   | **Collect/Choose**, **Clean/Filter**, and **Visualize** | 1              |
| 2   | **Generate New Information**                        | 2–3            |

_Tip: Explore a few datasets in the Data Visualizer before you pick one. Look for a chart that tells an interesting story, since it'll be easier to write about._

### How will you be graded?
Full credit looks like this for each step:

| Step                       | Full credit                                                                               |
|----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Collect/Choose Data**    | Your dataset is correctly named, and its description is complete.                         |
| **Clean/Filter Data**      | Your reason for cleaning and/or filtering, or not, is complete and reasonable.            |
| **Visualize Data**         | Your chart is readable and has a title and a citation.                                    |
| **Interpret**              | Your chart is thoroughly explained.                                                       |
| **Insights or Decisions**  | Your insights or decisions are reasonable and effectively linked to your chart.           |
| **Bias**                   | Possible problems with your analysis or potential bias are reasonable and thoughtfully addressed. |

## Assignment
{{% instructions-unit-journal-update %}}

### Make Your Chart (Day 1) (~40mins)
Choose a dataset in Level 1, and chart it:

1. **Collect/Choose:** pick a dataset from the Data Library, click "More info" to read its metadata, and write its name and a short description.
2. **Clean/Filter:** write whether you cleaned or filtered the data, and why or why not.
3. **Visualize:** make a chart in the Data Visualizer, then sketch it with a title and a citation of the dataset.

### Tell Your Data Story (Day 2) (~40mins)
Write about your chart in Level 2:

1. **Interpret:** write what information your chart shows.
2. **Insights or Decisions:** write what new insights or decisions someone could make based on your chart.
3. **Bias:** write any possible problems with your analysis, including potential bias in the dataset or your interpretation.

_Before you submit, check each step against the grading table above. Then finish the end-of-unit survey in Level 3._
